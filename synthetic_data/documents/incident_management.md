# Incident Management

## Evidence standards
All incident analysis must separate observed facts from hypotheses and recommendations. Do not present a hypothesis as a fact unless it is confirmed by strong evidence.

## Required workflow
Capture logs, compare the current execution to the previous successful run, check for known issues in the change log, and review documentation before escalating.

## Documentation requirement
Every conclusion must cite source evidence from logs, job history, or approved documentation. If evidence is missing, say that the evidence was not found.
